      Building a life in a new country is its own kind of labor, one that rarely gets named as hard even though it is. I'm Samar Farange, a licensed therapist, and I offer online therapy to immigrants and first-generation adults across Washington state.

      I work with people who left one place for another, whether that's recent or decades ago. This can include those who are raising families here while still carrying ties, obligations, and grief connected to where they came from.
  - type: list
    heading: Who this is for
    items:
      - Homesickness or grief for a place and life you left behind
      - The exhaustion of navigating systems of healthcare, school, and work that no one explained to you
      - Isolation from being far from the people who used to know you best
      - Guilt about building a life your family back home can't fully share
      - Being the point person for everyone else's paperwork, appointments, and translation
      - Reconciling who you were there with who you're becoming here
  - type: text
    heading: What sessions look like
    body: A lot of this work is about naming things that get treated as just logistics. Loneliness, loss, the sheer cognitive load of doing everything in a second language or a second system. These are real expectations that should start getting real attention. You won't have to explain your culture from scratch before we can get to the actual work.
