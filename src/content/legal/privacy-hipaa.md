  - type: "text"
    body: |
      This notice explains how your information is handled at Nour Therapy, a solo private practice. It covers both your protected health information under HIPAA if you're a client, and the limited information this website itself collects. I take this seriously — it's your story, not mine to pass around.
  - type: "text"
    heading: "Who sees your information"
    body: |
      There is no front desk, no billing department, no other clinician. I am the only person who has access to your records, session notes, and anything you share with me. I don't employ administrative staff, so your information isn't being handled by anyone other than me.
  - type: "list"
    heading: "How your health information is used"
    items:
      - "**Treatment** — to plan and provide your care, and to consult my own clinical judgment session to session."
      - "**Payment** — to process payment and, if you request one, to prepare a monthly superbill for your insurance reimbursement."
      - "**Legal requirements** — in the rare situations the law requires disclosure without your consent: suspected child or vulnerable-adult abuse, a serious and imminent threat to your safety or someone else's, or a valid court order."
      - "Outside of those situations, nothing about the fact that you're a client, or anything you've shared in a session, is disclosed to anyone without your written permission."
  - type: "list"
    heading: "Your rights"
    items:
      - "Request a copy of your records."
      - "Ask me to amend something in your records you believe is incorrect."
      - "Request that I communicate with you in a particular way or at a particular location."
      - "Ask for an accounting of certain disclosures of your information."
      - "File a complaint if you feel your privacy rights have been violated — with me directly, or with the U.S. Department of Health & Human Services Office for Civil Rights. Filing a complaint will never affect your care."
  - type: "text"
    heading: "Telehealth privacy"
    body: |
      Sessions happen over a private, encrypted video or phone platform — not a consumer video-chat app. I'll always be in a private space for our sessions, and I'd encourage you to find one too, since telehealth can't fully guarantee that no one else in your home can overhear you. Let me know if privacy on your end is hard to arrange; we can talk through it.
  - type: "text"
    heading: "This website"
    body: |
      This site doesn't run advertising trackers or analytics software, and it doesn't use cookies of its own. The only third-party service embedded on the site is Calendly, used for the booking widget on the contact section — scheduling through it is subject to [Calendly's own privacy policy](https://calendly.com/privacy). My website host keeps basic, routine server logs (like any website), which I don't access for anything beyond keeping the site running.
  - type: "text"
    heading: "Questions or concerns"
    body: |
      If anything here is unclear, or you want to raise a concern about how your information has been handled, call me at (425) 409-6557. You can also file a complaint with the HHS Office for Civil Rights at [www.hhs.gov/ocr/complaints](https://www.hhs.gov/ocr/complaints) — I will never retaliate against you for filing one.
